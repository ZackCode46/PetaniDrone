# Smart Drone Farm — FIRST RELEASE

**Smart Drone Farm** is a farming automation game inspired by the concept of *The Farmer Was Replaced*. In this game, players control drones to plant, water, grow, and harvest crops while managing resources and expanding their farm.

The main concept of the game combines **farming simulation, drone automation, and programming logic**.

## Features

* Drone movement and control
* Planting system
* Crop growth system
* Watering system
* Harvesting system
* Multiple crop types
* Coin and economy system
* Shop and upgrades
* Farm expansion
* Multiple drones
* Simulation speed control
* Automation-based gameplay

## Gameplay

Start with a small farm and use your drone to manage the available land.

Plant crops, take care of them, harvest them, and sell the results to earn coins. Use your earnings to purchase upgrades, unlock new crops, expand your farm, and increase the number of drones available.

The goal is to gradually build a more efficient and automated farm.

## Crops

The first release currently includes:

* Wheat
* Carrot

Each crop has different growth times and values, making resource management and farm optimization an important part of the gameplay.
